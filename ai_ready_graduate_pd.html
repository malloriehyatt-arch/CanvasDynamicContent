Profile of an AI-Ready Graduate

Helping teachers use AI to enhance (not replace) essential student skills like creativity, critical thinking, and agency.

Instructions & Facilitation Guide

How to facilitate or navigate this interactive professional development activity.

Module 1

6-Role Matrix

Explore the 6 graduate roles (Learner, Researcher, Synthesizer, Problem-Solver, Connector, Storyteller) dynamically tailored by Grade Band.

Module 2

Human vs. AI Card Sort

Sort educational tasks into Human Responsibility vs. AI Augmentation based on ISTE standards and student agency.

Module 3

Scenario Matrix

Analyze practical classroom situations to identify which profile role is being fostered in real-world learning.

🎯 Self-Paced Individual Exploration (15–20 Mins)

Navigate through Sections 1 to 3 at your own pace. Complete the micro-scenarios and card sorting to reflect on how AI can be integrated into your subject area while protecting student agency.

👥 PLC & Department Workshop Facilitation (30–45 Mins)

  • Section 1 (10 mins): Select your target grade level. Each teacher selects a role and shares how it aligns with current unit plans.
  • Section 2 (15 mins): Work in pairs to sort tasks. Debate gray areas (e.g., fact-checking vs. brainstorming).
  • Section 3 (10 mins): Classify classroom scenarios together and discuss changes you can make to upcoming projects.

Reflective Questions for Educators:

  1. Which profile role do your current classroom tasks support most naturally? Which role needs more attention?
  2. How can we ensure students use AI to extend critical thinking rather than bypass it?

Goal:

Distinguish tasks requiring uniquely human judgment and personal agency from tasks appropriate for AI augmentation based on ISTE standards.
