How It Works

Understanding the 3 Colors

Just like a traffic light, each color tells students clearly when and how tech tools can be used. Click any color below to read what it means for students and see easy assignment ideas.

🔴 No AI Allowed

Red Light

100% Student's Own Work

What Students Do

Complete work entirely on their own using their own brainpower. AI tools are strictly off-limits.

Why Use It?

To check what students truly know on their own, test memory, build core skills, and read personal thoughts.

Great Examples

  • In-class quizzes and tests
  • Personal journal entries and opinion pieces
  • Basic skill practice (spelling, math steps)
  • In-class timed speeches or essays
🟡 Classroom Assistant

Yellow Light

Ask First & Show How It Helped

What Students Do

Get teacher permission first, then use AI like a helpful classmate for ideas, spelling checks, or feedback.

Why Use It?

Helps students practice getting feedback, improving their first drafts, and brainstorming project topics.

Great Examples

  • Brainstorming topics for a science project
  • Getting grammar or style tips on a draft
  • Making outlines before writing an essay
  • Asking AI to explain a confusing concept
🟢 Full Collaboration

Green Light

Explore & Create Together

What Students Do

Actively use AI as a main learning tool to build, design, or explore. Students guide AI and check its answers for accuracy.

Why Use It?

Teaches critical thinking, double-checking facts, asking smart questions, and creative problem-solving.

Great Examples

  • Co-writing creative stories or character scripts
  • Building computer games or coding projects
  • Using AI as a practice quiz partner or tutor
  • Analyzing large amounts of information
